WHOOPDY DOO

Ride. Reset. Go again.

Whoopdy Doo is a landscape mountain-bike game for iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 17 or later.

Find your line

Choose Free Ride to explore or pick a challenge trail to chase six gates and a medal. The question-mark button opens How to ride. You can also open the guide while paused.

Controls

Use the left stick to steer and shift your weight. In the air, hold it sideways to spin, forward to frontflip or back to backflip. Release or countersteer to align before landing. Small forward and back movements adjust your balance.

Hold Pedal to build speed on the ground. Press Pump on a downhill face to gain speed; hold and release to hop. Hold it in the air to brace. A fresh forward compression on a rising ramp can keep a jump lower.

The handlebar above Jump controls both brakes. Touch the middle for both, slide left for the front brake or right for the rear. Front braking transfers weight forward; a locked rear tire can slide. Lift your finger to release.

After a crash, the rider and bike keep moving. Wait for the wreck to settle, then choose Back on the bike.

Explore and progress

The mountain map lets you pin destinations and take summit lifts. Look for six landmarks, connecting routes and marked gap lines. Trail Journal shows riding goals and cosmetic rider kits.

Progress, settings and clean-line ghosts stay on your device. There is no account or cloud recovery. Deleting the app removes local game data, subject to device backups.

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Your game stays on your device

Whoopdy Doo stores game progress, settings and best-line recordings locally on your device. It does not require an account and does not send those records to a server.

The current version contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs that collect player data. It does not request access to contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

Device backups and Apple

Deleting the app removes local game data, subject to backups managed by your device and Apple. Apple may process App Store and diagnostic information under its own privacy policy and your device settings.

When you ask for help

If you email Tim Hudson Studio at timhudsonstudio@gmail.com, the information you choose to include is used to respond to your request.

This website

These support pages use no cookies, analytics scripts or advertising. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process technical request information to deliver it. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Free · iOS/iPadOS 17 or later · Landscape play · No account required

THREE WAYS DOWN

Learn a line.

Flow through Fern & Fable, thread the turns of Loose Goose, or take to the air on Cloud Nine. Ride through six gates, chase a medal and improve your best clean-line ghost.

FREE RIDE

Follow your curiosity.

Explore the mountain map, find six hidden places and follow connecting routes. Look for marked gap lines, then take a summit lift for another descent.

FIND YOUR BALANCE

Make the landing.

Shift your weight with the stick, control spins and somersaults, and choose your front or rear brake balance. Crashes keep rolling. Let the wreck settle, then get back on the bike.

YOUR NEXT RUN

Keep a trail journal.

Work toward nine riding, air and exploration goals to earn rider kits. Progress and best lines stay on your device.

What makes a good mountain-bike game for you?

Learning a trail, linking tricks, procedural downhill runs and racing all offer different kinds of riding. Our guide compares Whoopdy Doo with Shred! 2, Descenders and Bike Unchained 3 using their public App Store features.

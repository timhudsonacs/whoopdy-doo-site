MUNTED: MTB Downhill mountain biking for iPhone

Pump through rollers, land a clean jump and see if you can beat your own ghost on the way down.

Download free on the App Store ↗

Currently listed as Whoopdy Doo · iPhone and iPad with iOS/iPadOS 17+ · No account required

Three downhill trails

Start with the flow of Fern & Fable, thread the tighter turns on Loose Goose, or go for bigger air on Cloud Nine. Ride through six gates, earn a medal and try to beat your best clean-line ghost.

Free Ride

Find six hidden places, follow connecting routes and look for marked gap lines. Take the summit lift when you’re ready for another descent.

Pump, hop and land

Use Pump / Scrub on rollers, charge a hop for more height, and shift your weight to bring both wheels back under you. Front and rear brake balance gives you another way to set up a turn.

Trail journal

Work toward nine riding, air and exploration goals to earn rider kits. Your progress and best lines stay on your device.

Take your first run

Start on Fern & Fable and aim to get through all six gates. On your next descent, pick one roller to pump and one landing to clean up.
